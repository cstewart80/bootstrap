Robyn Stewart named Legacy Producer

The flight attendant at the center of the award-winning documentary joins its feature expansion with a producer credit she has already earned.

Legacy Producer55 yearsSince 1971

Gwingz Studios today announced that Robyn Stewart, an American Airlines flight attendant since 1971, has been named Legacy Producer on Golden Wings: Stewardess to Sky Queen, the feature-length expansion of the award-winning documentary short about her career and her family's three generations in commercial aviation.

The credit is not a courtesy. Stewart located Jock Bethune, the longtime "voice of American Airlines" who worked alongside her father on the 747 training program, and brought him to the film. She pointed the production toward Millie Alford, founder of the American Airlines Stewardess College, where Stewart herself trained in 1971.

747
The entry point

Stewart's father, Jay R. Ricks, helped build the airline's 747 training program ahead of the aircraft's launch. A few years later his daughter became one of the first flight attendants trained to fly on it. The film uses the airplane as a bridge between two careers that together span roughly 88 years of service at a single airline.

“

When she said 'you should talk to Jock,' she was not making a suggestion, she was producing. The credit just makes it official.

— Caleb Mills Stewart, Director
“

Fifty-five years and I still get on the airplane. Millie Alford pinned wings on girls like me and sent us out into the world. Jock worked beside my father. I have been carrying these people with me my whole career. Now they get to be on screen, and I get a say in how.

— Robyn Stewart, Legacy Producer
