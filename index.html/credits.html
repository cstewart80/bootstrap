Credits

Key subjects & crew

Robyn Stewart

Legacy Producer
Principal subject

An American Airlines flight attendant since 1971. Trained under Millie Alford at the Stewardess College; among the first flight attendants trained to fly the 747. Marked 55 years of service in July 2026.

Caleb Mills Stewart

Director
Filmmaker, Gwingz Studios

Director of Golden Wings: Stewardess to Sky Queen, a feature expanded from the short Golden Wings: Fifty Year Flight Path.

Jock Bethune

Interviewee
Longtime "voice of American Airlines"

Worked alongside Jay R. Ricks on the 747 training program. His on-camera interview at the C.R. Smith Museum anchors the feature.

Jay R. Ricks

Key Subject
WWII aviator, American Airlines flight engineer

Helped build the airline's 747 training program ahead of the aircraft's launch. Robyn Stewart's father.

Millie Alford

Key Subject
Director of American Airlines Stewardess College

In November 1958, with C.R. Smith (Founding President of American Airlines) attending, Rose Mildred "Millie" Alford presided over the opening of the American Airlines Stewardess College. Over the next decade, the school trained thousands of flight attendants, including Robyn Stewart.

C.R. Smith

Key Subject
Founding President of American Airlines

Cyrus Rowlett "C.R." Smith led American Airlines from 1934 to 1968 and later served as U.S. Secretary of Commerce under President Lyndon B. Johnson. He attended the 1958 opening of the American Airlines Stewardess College. The C.R. Smith Museum, where Jock Bethune's interview was filmed, bears his name.

Lulu Stewart

Interviewee
On camera with Robyn Stewart

Sits beside Robyn Stewart in every interview scene, sharing the conversation as Robyn looks back on more than five decades in the air.
