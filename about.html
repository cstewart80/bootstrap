About the film

Golden Wings: Stewardess to Sky Queen is a feature documentary in development from Gwingz Studios, directed by Caleb Mills Stewart. It expands the award-winning short Golden Wings: Fifty Year Flight Path into a portrait of one American Airlines family across three generations, from the 747 rollout to the present day, told through interviews, family archives, and synthetic-media reconstruction where period footage was lost.

Fifty-five years and I still get on the airplane.

— Robyn Stewart, Legacy Producer

The short began as a student project at Academy of Art University, was shot on a smartphone, and went on to win more than a dozen festival awards, including Best Short Documentary at the International Guadalajara Film Awards and the Swedish International Film Festival, and Best Short Cinematography at the Silicon Beach Film Festival, where it premiered at the TCL Chinese Theatre in Los Angeles.
